ASHEN DEPTHS — COMPLETE WEAPON & BLESSING REWORK
Version 3.1

8 WEAPONS | 59 BLESSINGS | 8 WEAPON EVOLUTIONS

========================================
WEAPONS (8)
========================================

1. CINDERWAKE — ASHEN GREATSWORD
Heavy, slow, sweeping melee weapon.
- Three-hit combo ending in a flame wave.
- Aerial attack slams into the ground, creating a shockwave.
- Perfectly timed charged strikes deal additional stagger damage.
- Hitting burning enemies with a Perfect Strike consumes Burn to create an explosion.
Evolution: WORLDPYRE — Charged attacks send larger flame waves and leave temporary fire trails.
Weakness: Long attack recovery and vulnerability to interruption.

2. BLOODLETTER — TWIN RITUAL DAGGERS
Extremely fast, close-range Bleed weapon.
- Five-hit rapid combo that applies Bleed.
- Descending cross-slash deals extra damage against bleeding enemies.
- Charged stab advances through minor enemies.
- Dodging through an enemy empowers the next backstab.
Evolution: RED HARVEST — Executing bleeding enemies creates temporary blood shades that repeat attacks.
Weakness: Very short reach and limited stagger.

3. MOURNING REAPER — SPECTRAL SCYTHE
Wide sweeping attacks and soul collection.
- Broad combo ending in a rising slash.
- Aerial attack strikes multiple nearby targets.
- Charged attacks release spectral crescents.
- Kills collect souls; five souls empower the next heavy attack.
Evolution: FUNERAL PROCESSION — Spending souls creates multiple spectral slashes.
Weakness: Lower single-target damage without souls.

4. GRAVEHOOK — CHAIN SICKLE
Extreme reach and aerial positioning.
- Alternates short sickle attacks with ranged chain swings.
- Hooks lighter enemies toward you or pulls you toward heavier enemies.
- Aerial hooks can suspend minor enemies.
- Successful hooks empower follow-up aerial attacks.
Evolution: IRON TEMPEST — Hooks enable spinning chain attacks that damage surrounding enemies.
Weakness: Punishing missed attacks and vulnerable recovery.

5. OATHBREAKER — ANCIENT WARHAMMER
Massive stagger and armor-breaking attacks.
- Heavy three-hit combo ending in a crushing strike.
- Aerial ground slam produces an impact wave.
- Charged attacks crack the ground and launch minor enemies.
- Repeated heavy strikes fracture enemy armor.
Evolution: RUINMAKER — Charged strikes create delayed aftershocks and spread armor fractures.
Weakness: Extremely slow attacks and recovery.

6. HOLLOWPOINT — DUELIST'S RAPIER
Precision, critical strikes, and counters.
- Narrow four-hit thrust combo.
- Aerial thrust maintains forward movement.
- Consecutive accurate hits build Focus.
- Perfect dodges allow empowered counterthrusts.
Evolution: PERFECT THESIS — Maximum-Focus counters expose temporary weak points.
Weakness: Poor crowd coverage and high accuracy requirements.

7. WRAITHSTRING — SPIRIT BOW
Ranged precision and piercing arrows.
- Charged arrows pierce multiple enemies.
- Aiming in the air briefly slows falling.
- Consecutive successful shots generate spectral follow-up arrows.
- Charged attacks prioritize precise positioning.
Evolution: GHOST CHOIR — Charged arrows create marks that release delayed spectral volleys.
Weakness: Vulnerable charging windows and aiming requirements.

8. ASHEN FISTS — EMBERFORGED GAUNTLETS
Very fast close-range brawling.
- Rapid punch combinations.
- Consecutive hits increase attack speed.
- Charged uppercuts launch smaller enemies.
- Perfect dodges enable empowered uppercuts.
Evolution: METEOR KNUCKLES — Uppercuts ignite enemies and aerial finishers cause explosions.
Weakness: Extremely short range and dangerous proximity.


========================================
OFFENSIVE BLESSINGS (12)
========================================

1. RUPTURE
Applying Bleed to an already bleeding enemy builds Rupture. At five stacks, consume the stacks to deal a burst of damage equal to 120% weapon damage.
Synergy: Bloodletter, Crimson Echo.

2. EMBERCHAIN
Hitting three different enemies within four seconds connects them with burning chains. Connected enemies share 25% of incoming direct attack damage for five seconds.
Synergy: Cinderwake, Mourning Reaper.

3. FINAL JUDGMENT
Heavy attacks against staggered enemies create a delayed impact that repeats 50% of the attack's damage.
Synergy: Oathbreaker, Stonebound.

4. SOUL REND
Killing an enemy with a charged attack releases a seeking spectral projectile dealing 70% weapon damage.
Synergy: Mourning Reaper, Wraithstring.

5. PLAGUEBEARER
Poisoned enemies release toxic clouds upon death. Clouds persist for four seconds and apply Poison to nearby enemies. Maximum three active clouds.
Synergy: Poison and area-control builds.

6. FROSTBITE
Repeated attacks build Chill. At six stacks, minor enemies freeze for 1.2 seconds. Bosses instead suffer a temporary 20% attack-speed reduction.
Synergy: Shatterpoint, fast weapons.

7. EXECUTIONER'S MERCY
Killing a staggered enemy releases a damaging shockwave equal to 75% weapon damage.
Synergy: Oathbreaker, Cinderwake.

8. CRIMSON ECHO
Every fourth primary melee strike repeats after a short delay at 40% damage.
Synergy: Bloodletter, Hollowpoint.

9. ASHEN BRAND
Heavy attacks ignite enemies. Killing a burning enemy transfers a reduced Burn effect to a nearby enemy.
Synergy: Cinderwake, Sovereign of Ash.

10. RELENTLESS EDGE
Consecutive primary attacks against the same enemy progressively increase attack speed, up to 20%. Missing or changing targets resets the bonus.
Synergy: Hollowpoint, Ashen Fists.

11. SHATTERPOINT
Heavy attacks against frozen enemies consume Freeze and create an area explosion. Bosses instead receive additional stagger damage when maximum Chill is consumed.
Synergy: Frostbite, Oathbreaker.

12. LAST RITES
Charged attacks deal 50% additional damage to normal enemies below 15% HP. Bosses receive a reduced 20% bonus.
Synergy: Wraithstring, Hollowpoint.


========================================
MOVEMENT BLESSINGS (12)
========================================

1. AFTERIMAGE
Dodging creates a decoy that attracts nearby enemies for 1.5 seconds. Six-second cooldown.

2. COMET DIVE
Attacking while falling at high speed creates an impact shockwave. Longer falls produce more damage, up to a cap.

3. WIND DANCER
Alternating successful grounded and aerial attacks grants 12% movement speed for five seconds.

4. REVERSAL
Perfectly dodging through an enemy turns you toward it and empowers the next counterattack by 35%.

5. ENDLESS ASCENT
Landing an aerial heavy attack grants a small upward boost. Can trigger twice before landing.

6. SHADOW PURSUIT
Killing an enemy while airborne empowers the next dodge, increasing its distance and allowing passage through enemies.

7. PHANTOM STEP
Perfect dodges create a spectral shade that repeats the next primary attack at 35% damage.

8. FALLING STAR
Downward aerial attacks gain bonus damage based on vertical distance fallen, up to 60%.

9. SKYBREAKER
Killing an airborne enemy restores one aerial jump, subject to a maximum airborne-jump limit.

10. GHOSTWALK
Dodging through an enemy marks it. Your next primary attack against that target deals 30% additional damage.

11. FEATHERLESS WINGS
Your second jump allows a brief controlled hover and improved horizontal repositioning.

12. PREDATOR'S RHYTHM
Landing three distinct primary attacks without taking damage restores 25% of dodge cooldown.


========================================
DEFENSIVE BLESSINGS (12)
========================================

1. ASHEN CARAPACE
Attacking while maintaining your position builds temporary armor. Up to three stacks. Dodging removes all accumulated armor.

2. GUARDIAN FLAME
Taking damage produces a ring of fire that pushes nearby minor enemies away. Eight-second cooldown.

3. UNDYING WILL
Below 25% health, ordinary damage can no longer interrupt attacks. Damage and death are still possible.

4. SOUL SHELTER
Killing three enemies without taking damage grants a temporary shield. Only one shield can exist at once.

5. PENANCE
Taking damage marks the responsible enemy for five seconds. Killing it restores a portion of the lost health, rounded appropriately for the mask system. Bosses allow a bounded damage-based recovery trigger.

6. STONEBOUND
Landing a fully charged heavy attack grants 20% damage resistance against the next hit for three seconds.

7. IRON RESOLVE
Taking a particularly powerful hit grants temporary damage resistance. Eighteen-second cooldown.

8. GRAVEKEEPER'S GIFT
Defeating an elite without using a healing charge awards a healing fragment. Fragments combine into a full mask.

9. VENGEFUL GUARD
Taking damage empowers your next heavy attack with bonus damage and stagger.

10. SECOND SKIN
Perfect dodges grant a small temporary protective shield. Only one shield may exist at a time.

11. DEATH'S REFUSAL
Once per run, survive a lethal hit at one remaining health mask and gain brief invulnerability. Does not stack with other revival effects.

12. UNBROKEN OATH
While at full health, gain greater resistance to interruption and stagger. The effect ends after losing health.


========================================
CURSED BLESSINGS (11)
========================================

A cursed blessing must provide a powerful advantage and an independent, meaningful disadvantage.

1. RAVENOUS BLADE
Benefit: Each enemy killed increases damage by 2%, up to 30%.
Curse: Taking damage removes half the accumulated bonus.
Proposed improvement: Also reduce base damage by 10%, making the drawback meaningful even when no kill stacks are active.

2. BORROWED TIME
Benefit: Gain 30% attack speed.
Curse: After every boss victory, permanently lose a portion of maximum health for the rest of the run.
Mask-system adjustment: Lose one maximum mask after every two bosses, subject to a minimum survivable maximum.

3. MARTYR'S FLAME
Benefit: Charged attacks become explosive.
Curse: Each charged attack consumes a portion of your healing resource. If none remains, it accumulates Blood Debt; reaching the debt threshold removes a mask.
Identity: Spend survival resources to increase offensive power.

4. FRAGILE DIVINITY
Benefit: Gain one additional regular blessing slot.
Curse: Enemies deal 20% more damage, with mask damage rounded using the game's actual damage rules.

5. HUNGER OF THE VOID
Benefit: Defeating an elite grants a temporary 35% damage increase and movement-speed bonus.
Curse: Ordinary enemies no longer drop healing resources.

6. THE UNFORGIVEN
Benefit: Critical primary attacks reduce active ability cooldowns.
Curse: Healing takes twice as long, creating larger vulnerable windows.

7. BLOOD COVENANT
Benefit: Deal 35% more direct attack damage.
Curse: Healing effectiveness is reduced. With a mask-based health system, every second completed healing action restores no mask but still consumes its required resource.

8. GLASS SAINT
Benefit: The bonus damage from critical hits is doubled.
Curse: Permanently lose two maximum health masks for the current run, subject to the game's minimum maximum health.

9. THE LAST FLAME
Benefit: At low health, attacks unleash progressively stronger flames.
Curse: While the empowered flames are active, they slowly drain a separate danger meter that removes a mask at its threshold. The danger meter is visible.

10. BURDEN OF KINGS
Benefit: Heavy attacks deal 60% more stagger damage.
Curse: Dodge recovery is 25% longer.

11. DEATH'S BARGAIN
Benefit: Gain an additional one-time revival.
Curse: Upon revival, permanently lose one maximum health mask for the remainder of the run. This penalty must remain meaningful after revival.


========================================
LEGENDARY BLESSINGS (12)
========================================

1. ECLIPSE
After 20 successful primary hits, enter Eclipse for six seconds. Primary attacks launch dark spectral slashes.
Drawback: Healing is disabled during Eclipse.

2. THE SEVENTH BELL
Every seventh consecutive successful primary attack summons a massive spectral strike. Missing or taking damage resets the count.

3. PHOENIX SOUL
Once per run, lethal damage causes a fiery resurrection that restores a portion of your health masks and ignites nearby enemies.

4. THOUSANDFOLD BLADE
Every third primary attack creates two spectral copies dealing 30% damage each. Copies cannot trigger more copies.

5. SOVEREIGN OF ASH
Attacking burning enemies occasionally releases homing embers. Killing three burning enemies empowers the next heavy attack into a firestorm.

6. THE ENDLESS HUNT
Killing an enemy marks the next enemy as Prey. Prey receives increased stagger damage. Killing Prey renews the mark and grants temporary movement speed.

7. CROWN OF THE FALLEN
Every fifth enemy killed summons a spectral knight that fights alongside you for 12 seconds. One knight maximum.

8. HOURGLASS OF RUIN
Perfect dodges briefly slow nearby enemies while you retain normal attack speed. Bosses experience reduced slowing.

9. HEART OF THE ABYSS
Once per run, lethal damage consumes all remaining healing charges to revive you. The amount of health restored depends on charges consumed.

10. WINGS OF THE FORSAKEN
Gain a third aerial jump. Aerial attacks following that jump summon falling spectral blades.
Drawback: Grounded attacks deal 10% less damage.

11. PERFECT STORM
Primary attacks after perfect dodges generate elemental charges. At three charges, the next heavy attack releases a combined lightning-and-wind storm.

12. THE HOLLOW KING
Defeating an elite summons a temporary spectral copy of it. The copy fights for you using a simplified signature attack. One summon maximum.

========================================
SYSTEM & BALANCING RULES
========================================

- One active weapon per run.
- Six regular blessing slots.
- One legendary blessing slot.
- One optional cursed blessing slot.
- Weapon evolution does not consume a blessing slot.
- Blessings must offer meaningful gameplay changes, not simply numerical bonuses.
- Cursed blessings must have disadvantages independent from their benefits.
- Every weapon must remain viable without a required blessing.
- Evolutions may be unlocked through blessing combinations or weapon-mastery alternatives.
- Secondary attacks cannot recursively trigger themselves.
- Summoned attacks, explosions, echoes, and status ticks should not generate infinite chains.
- Bosses resist repeated crowd-control effects but remain vulnerable to appropriate alternative blessing effects.
- Percentage-based healing must be adapted to the mask-based health system.
- Temporary shields must have clear rules separate from health masks.
- Damage, stagger, status duration, and critical effects need sensible caps.
- Early rewards establish a build direction.
- Midgame rewards develop existing synergies.
- Late-game rewards allow evolution, refinement, or replacement.
- At full capacity, players explicitly choose what to replace.
- No randomly deleting blessings to manufacture difficulty.
- Every blessing should have recognizable visual feedback.
- Test every weapon against crowds, elites, ranged enemies, and bosses.
- All numerical balance values remain provisional until gameplay testing.
